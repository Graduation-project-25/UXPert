Suggestions

Here are specific actions you can take to improve your heuristic rules, enhance design consistency, and usability:

  • Adopt a Consistent Color Palette:

    Choose a color palette that reflects your brand identity and use it consistently across all frames. Tools like Adobe Color or Coolors can help.

  • Align Elements for Better Visual Hierarchy:

    Use guides and grids to align elements. Ensure uniform spacing between items for a clean layout.

  • Adjust Sizes for Proportional Relationships:

    Maintain scale consistency between UI components to establish unity in design.

  • Incorporate Whitespace Effectively:

    Increase whitespace around text and images to reduce cognitive load. Ensure at least 20% of your layout is whitespace.

  • Ensure Accessibility Compliance:

    Use contrast checkers like WebAIM to verify color contrast. Aim for at least a 4.5:1 ratio for body text.
